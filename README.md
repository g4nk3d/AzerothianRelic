# Azerothian Relic

A complete theme for **NetherUI**: ancient bronze reliquary, navy obsidian and polished lapis.

- **NetherUI:** panels, borders, window frames, title plates, buttons, tabs, widgets, bars,
  portrait rings, crests, minimap border, icons and NetherUI's Light, all replaced with the
  Relic's own artwork, plus the Relic colour palette.
- **NetherBars (optional):** the approved painted Relic plates for NetherBars' unit frames
  (player, target, focus, pet, target of target) and nameplates (normal, elite, rare), the
  horned elite bar with a chevron for elite, boss and rare targets, and the Triad Crown over
  your target. Needs NetherBars 3.0.26 or newer.

**All or none.** The Relic is one complete look. While it is NetherUI's theme, NetherBars uses
it for every kind of nameplate and for its unit frames too. Pick any other NetherUI theme and
nothing uses the Relic any more. Your own NetherBars choices are kept and come back when the
Relic is off.

## Use it
1. Install NetherUI (required) and, if you like, NetherBars.
2. Restart the game.
3. At your first login the Relic asks: *Would you like to allow Azerothian Relic to handle all
   UI/Nameplates/Unit Frames?* **Azerothian Relic** hands everything NetherUI and NetherBars draw
   to the Relic; **No** keeps everything as it is.
4. Change your mind any time: Options > AddOns > Azerothian Relic, `/relic on` / `/relic off`, or
   pick a theme in NetherUI's gallery. Turning it off gives NetherUI and NetherBars back exactly
   what they had before.

`/relic` shows the version and whether it is on. Nothing in NetherUI's or NetherBars' files is
changed.
