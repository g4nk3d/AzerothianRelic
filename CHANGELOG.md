# Azerothian Relic changelog


## 1.0.0
- First release: the Azerothian Relic theme for NetherUI (176 NetherUI textures replaced,
  37 extra pieces registered by name, the Relic palette) and the matching look for NetherBars'
  nameplates and unit frames. Needs NetherBars 3.0.26 or newer for the painted plates.
- Unit frames are the approved painted Relic plates: your ring plate, the same plate facing you
  for your target, and their own plates for focus, pet and target of target. Bars sit inside the
  plate's rims, health and resource numbers at the right end, the level under the portrait.
- Elite, boss, rare and rare-elite targets wear the horned elite bar; the chevron around the
  portrait tells them apart.
- Nameplates are the painted Relic plates (normal, elite and rare), with the Triad Crown floating
  over your target. Friendly players show their name, and their plate while they are in combat.
- The square minimap border is sized to frame the map, overlapping its edge by 2 pixels.
- Quest and dialogue windows keep their buttons (Accept, Decline, Complete) clear of the big
  Relic corners.
- Tooltips keep their text clear of the thick bronze border (needs NetherUI 2.0.29).
- All or none: while the Relic is NetherUI's theme, NetherBars uses it everywhere too; with any
  other theme nothing uses it.
- Asks at first login whether to take over all UI, nameplates and unit frames; one switch in
  Options > AddOns > Azerothian Relic; turning it off restores your previous NetherUI theme and
  NetherBars' own choices exactly. /relic on, /relic off, /relic ask, /relic (status).
